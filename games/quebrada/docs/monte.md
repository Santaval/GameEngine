# Monte sheet (`tilesets/monte.png`, `monte.tsx`): the monte de hule y níspero and the aerogeneradores

Sixth tileset of `maps/zona1.tmx` (after town, buildings, park, school and ebais), generated by `tools/tilegen/catalog_monte.mjs` (`make tiles` / `node tools/tilegen/gen.mjs`).
`firstgid` = 1201 = 144 (town) + 384 (buildings) + 192 (park) + 224 (school) + 256 (ebais) + 1, computed in `zona1_map.mjs` as `MONTE_FIRSTGID` (previous firstgid + previous capacity), never typed.
Sheet: 16 x 16 tiles of 16 px = 256 slots, **161 used** (ids 0-160; the rest is reserved/transparent). First-pass procedural art, stylised and compressed (shapes and colours after
`docs/reference/aerogeneradores/*.jpg`: white three-blade turbines on grassy hills beside a road, and `docs/reference/monte/*.jpg`: the dense green monte). Every tile name starts with `monte_`
(none repeats a town name: the town's own `monte_trunk` / `monte_canopy_*` keep resolving to the town sheet). Property scheme as `town.tsx`: `name`, `layer` (`ground|overlay|ysort|above`),
`surface` only on non-solid ground, `solid` only on `ysort`, `sort_offset`. `tests/monte_check.lua` validates it. Context and coordinates: [zona1.md](zona1.md) (section "The monte").

The map uses the town's `guanacaste`, `palm` and `monte` trees besides the hule and the níspero, and the town's `grass_*` for the clearings.

## Tree anatomy

- **hule** (rubber tree): trunk cell (`ysort`, solid) at the base cell `(cx, cy)`, the straight upper trunk `monte_hule_shaft` (`above`) at `(cx, cy-1)` and the 3x3 canopy at `(cx-1..cx+1, cy-4..cy-2)`: 5 cells tall.
- **níspero**: like the town trees: trunk at `(cx, cy)`, 3x2 canopy at `(cx-1..cx+1, cy-2..cy-1)`.
- Trunks never touch each other (not even diagonally; same for bushes, logs, rocks and the turbine base), so no pocket can be closed and the flood fill in `gen.mjs` is a hard guarantee.

## Turbine anatomy (aerogenerador)

A turbine is the `turbine` element `{ id, kind: "turbine", at: [bx, by] }` (`by` = row of the base cell). It writes:

| Part | Cells | Layer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| pad (`monte_pad_a/b`, `concreto`) | cols bx-1..bx+1, rows by..by+1 (3 x 2) | `ground` |
| base `monte_tower_0_9` (door, shadow) | (bx, by): the only solid cell | `objects` (ysort) |
| shaft `monte_tower_0_1..8` | (bx, by-8 .. by-1) | `canopy` (above: the hero walks behind the tower) |
| nacelle `monte_tower_0_0` | (bx, by-9) | `canopy` |
| rotor: 9 x 9 cells (144 px, 3 blades), the hub = centre cell `monte_rotor_4_4` right over the nacelle | cols bx-4..bx+4, rows by-13..by-5 | `rotors` (its own above layer) |
| warning light `luz_<id>` | at the nacelle top, (bx*16+8, (by-9)*16+1) px | lights: red (255, 50, 40), radius 20, intensity 0.9, flicker 0.35, `only=dusk,night` |

Total height 14 rows above the base (tower 10 cells = 160 px, rotor top 13 rows over the base). The tower is 1 cell wide: only the base cell collides.

**Animated rotor: yes.** The engine supports tile animation (`TmxLoader` reads the tile's `<animation>`, `TileMap::displayGid` swaps the gid by the map clock; `engine/docs/lua-api.md`, `map.tilesets[i].tiles[n].animation`).
Each of the 57 non-empty rotor cells has an anchor tile (`monte_rotor_<cx>_<cy>`, the angle-0 picture) whose `<animation>` lists 4 frames of 400 ms: itself, then the cell at 30, 60 and 90 degrees (`..._f1`, `_f2`, `_f3`, or
`monte_rotor_blank` when that cell is empty at that angle). 3-blade symmetry makes 120 degrees a full cycle: 4 frames x 30 degrees, **one cycle every 1.6 s (about 12.5 rpm)**. The rotor lives in its own layer `rotors`
(above) so that the large static `canopy` layer is not flagged as animated (the engine redraws animated layers). All the turbines animate in sync. To change the speed edit `ROTOR_MS`; for a smoother turn raise `ROTOR_FRAMES` /
lower `ROTOR_STEP` (the 120 degree cycle must stay `ROTOR_FRAMES * ROTOR_STEP`) and check that the sheet still fits in 256 slots.

## Moving or adding turbines

- **Move / add**: edit `TURBINE_AT` in `zona1_map.mjs` (base `[col, row]`; every other thing derives: pad, tower, rotor, light `luz_aerogenerador_<n>`, the clearing in the forest, the `aerogeneradores` zone rect `TURBINE_BBOX`, the reachability checks).
  Keep `row >= 13` (the rotor top is 13 rows above the base; the generator throws otherwise), keep the clearing (`CLEAR_RX` = 7, `CLEAR_RY` = 11, an ellipse centred 3 rows above the base) free of other objects, and keep the turbines out of the road corridor
  (a base set back < 11 rows south of the road lets the blades hang over the road: fine for the game, only a look). Then update `tests/zona1_check.lua` (`TURBINES`, counts) and run `node tools/tilegen/gen.mjs`.
- **Redraw**: `drawTower` / `drawRotor` in `catalog_monte.mjs` (keep the tower 1 x 10 cells and the rotor 9 x 9 with the hub in the centre cell, or the layout constants `TOWER_H`, `ROTOR_N` and the placement change).
- **Trees / forest**: the `monte` `forest` element in `ELEMENTS` (`trees` weights, `density`, `props`, `ferns`, `ramp`, `verge`); see zona1.md.

`zona1_monte_x1.png` (the whole monte) and `zona1_monte_arboles_x2.png`. `gen.mjs` prints the monte distance (250 t = 500 m, throws otherwise), tree counts, density and the reachability result.

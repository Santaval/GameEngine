# Vida sheet (`tilesets/vida.png`, `vida.tsx`)

Eighth tileset of `maps/zona1.tmx`, appended after `rio` so no earlier firstgid moves (`VIDA_FIRSTGID` in `zona1_map.mjs`, computed). Generated by `tools/tilegen/catalog_vida.mjs` (`make tiles`).
Sheet: 16 x 16 tiles of 16 px (256 ids). Every tile name starts with `vida_`. Property scheme as `town.tsx` (`name`, `layer`, `surface` only on walkable ground, `solid` only on `ysort`, `sort_offset`, optional animation).
`tests/vida_check.lua` validates it.

| Ids | Content |
| --- | --- |
| 0-63 | name plaques `vida_rotulo_<key>_<cx>_0` (ysort, solid), 1 row tall and 3-4 tiles wide, text with the shared 3x5 pixel font (`tools/tilegen/font.mjs`) |
| 64-77 | animals: `vida_gallina_a/_b` (+ `_f1` peck frames), `vida_gallo` (+ `_f1`), `vida_perro_echado`, `vida_perro_sentado`, `vida_vaca_pinta/_negra` and `vida_caballo` (2x1, cells `_0_0`, `_1_0`) |
| 78-255 | reserved for street furniture (bus stop, laundry, moto, poles, signs) |

## Name plaques

`PLAQUES` in `catalog_vida.mjs`: `{ key, lines, style }`. Style `post` is a cream board on two wooden posts (one line); style `fence` is a board hung on a wire fence (up to two lines). The width is
`ceil((widest line in px + 4) / 16)` tiles (the font advances 4 px per character), at most 4, otherwise the generator throws. Plaques are placed with an `object` element (`name: "vida_rotulo_<key>"`).
Keys: `berta`, `maria_juvenal`, `isabel`, `carmen`, `machillos`, `ingracia`, `guarderia`, `caliles`, `enrique`, `paula`. Accents are dropped by the font (DONA, TIO), only the Ñ is kept.

### Where the plaques stand (`PLAQUE_AT`, exported to `tests/zona1_consts.lua`)

On the verge row beside each footpath (north verge row `SIDEWALK_N_Y - 1`, south `SIDEWALK_S_Y + 1`), never on a footpath column: `berta` (`BERTA_X + 4`), `maria_juvenal` (`AB_X + 12`), `isabel` (`BAR_X + 15`), `carmen` (`BAR_X + 35`),
`machillos` (`BAR_X + 44`) on the north side; `ingracia` (`AB_X + 4`), `guarderia` (`BAR_X + 6`), `caliles` (`CALILES_X + 4`), `enrique` (`ENR_LANE_X + 2`) on the south side; `paula` hangs in the yard fence beside the gate
(the fence skips those four cells). `tests/zona1_check.lua` checks every plaque's cells and that none sits on a footpath.

## Animals

Hens and the rooster are animated through `anim` (tile ids of this sheet, see `tsx.mjs`): hen A shows its peck frame in step 2 of 4 and hen B in step 3, so two hens do not move in lockstep (every copy of one anchor tile shares the clock);
the rooster crows (frame 2) every fourth 700 ms step. They are `ysort` without `solid`; dogs, cows and the horse are `ysort` and `solid`.
Placement (`ANIMAL_ELEMENTS` in `zona1_map.mjs`): three hens and a rooster plus a sitting dog in Abuela Paula's yard, three hens in Tío Enrique's yard, a lying dog beside the abastecedor and a sitting dog beside the bar, three cows and a horse in the potrero.
`tests/vida_check.lua` checks the animation frames and the solidity of every animal.

## Street furniture (ids 78+)

Multi-cell objects, upper rows `above` (you walk behind roofs, clothes and cables), the bottom row `ysort` and `solid` (posts, bench, moto): `vida_parada` (3x2 bus stop with a PARADA board and a bench), `vida_ropa_a` / `vida_ropa_b` (2x2 laundry lines),
`vida_moto` (2x1 parked motorbike), `vida_poste` (1x3 utility pole without a lamp, cable at its row y=2 like the lamp poles) and `vida_senal_alto` / `vida_senal_escolar` (1x2 ALTO board and school-zone diamond).
Placement (`FURNITURE_ELEMENTS`): the bus stop on the sidewalk / south verge beside the colegio's gate, a laundry line each between Doña Isabel's and Carmen's houses and between Carmen's and Los Machillos, the moto in front of María y Juvenal's, the school-zone sign before the colegio,
the ALTO sign at the mouth of Los Caliles, and one pole on the south verge (`POLE_X`, where the lamp column is skipped) that the power cable continues over: `wire` ends may be lamps or `vida_poste` objects.
The wooden pasture gate of the original plan was not drawn: the potrero's gate is the fence's open-gate tile (`fence_gap`).

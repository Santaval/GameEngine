## Scale

COMPRESSED scale: **2 m = 1 tile** (16 px = 2 m, 8 px = 1 m; `METRES_PER_TILE = 2` in `zona1_map.mjs`, also the
map property `metres_per_tile`). The real 100 m paver street is therefore **50 tiles = 800 px**. The walker moves
60 px/s = 3.75 tiles/s = **7.5 m/s** in game terms (running 100 px/s = 6.25 tiles/s = **12.5 m/s**), so the street
takes about **13.3 s** to walk end to end (8 s running).

Known distortions of the compression: the street is still 4 tiles wide (~8 m real, a real street is narrower;
kept so it stays comfortable to walk) and the church keeps its 10-tile drawing, i.e. ~20 m wide at this scale
(and 12 tiles = ~24 m deep). Distances along the street are the faithful part.

## Adding elements (workflow)

Everything is the `ELEMENTS` array near the top of `zona1_map.mjs`. Add an entry, run `node tools/tilegen/gen.mjs`
(then `--preview DIR` to look at `zona1.png`, `zona1_night.png`, `zona1_triggers.png`). Kinds:

- `street` / `path`: ground rectangles (`surface: "paver" | "lastre" | "asphalt" | "sidewalk"`; lastre autotiles against grass). `street` is horizontal (`length` along x) unless `dir: "v"`; asphalt streets take `line` (centre line column) and `gaps` (row ranges without line).
- `building`: `{ name, at: [tx, ty] }` for any building of the buildings, school or ebais tilesets (iglesia, casa_1/2/3, casa_mama, escuela_aulas_a/b, ebais_edificio);
  walls, roof layer, roof rect, footprint, lit windows and window lights come with it. `hedges` adds the church hedges.
- `tree` (`monte | guanacaste | platano | palm | hule | nispero`) and `prop` (weeds, rock, bench, stump...): on a free grass tile.
- Park kinds: `fill` (rectangle of ground tiles from any sheet, picked by position noise), `stamp` (list of single tiles on `ground` or `objects`),
  `court_lines` (boundary, centre line, boxes and the centre circle, joined by neighbours), `object` (a multi-cell object of the park sheet, e.g. `park_swing`; top-left anchor),
  `diag` (staircase path), `tree_zone` (seeded tree scatter on free grass), `scatter` (seeded props on free grass), `lamp` (`poste` + a dusk light; `soft` skips it if the cell is not free grass).
  Trees, lamps and equipment on occupied cells throw at generation time.
- Monte kinds: `road` (`{ x, y, length, width }`: asphalt road, edge / line tiles chosen by row; the last column is the junction), `forest` (`{ x0, x1, y0, y1, ramp, verge, seed, trees: [[kind, weight]], density, props, ferns }`: the seeded forest; it avoids road corridors, `turbine` clearings and `street` elements with `trail: true`),
  `turbine` (`{ at: [bx, by] }`: pad, tower, animated rotor, warning light; see [monte.md](monte.md)).
- `spawn`, `edge`, `zone`, `interactable`: object-layer entries in px.

More kinds: `door` (class `door`, props), `emitter` (point object), `surface` (rect with `surface`). The route, the triggers and the walkability check are code in `zona1_map.mjs`
(`ROUTE`, `routeLength()`, `pointAt(d)`, `roadRects()`, `triggerDefs()`).

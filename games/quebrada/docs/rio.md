# Rio sheet (`tilesets/rio.png`, `rio.tsx`) and the quebrada of zone 1

Seventh tileset of `maps/zona1.tmx` (after town, buildings, park, school, ebais and monte), generated by `tools/tilegen/catalog_rio.mjs` (`make tiles` / `node tools/tilegen/gen.mjs`); the course and the water mask come from `tools/tilegen/river.mjs`.
`firstgid` = 1457 = 1201 (monte) + 256 (monte capacity), computed in `zona1_map.mjs` as `RIO_FIRSTGID`, never typed. Sheet: 16 x 4 tiles = 64 slots, **19 used** (ids 0-18). Every name starts with `rio_`.
Property scheme as `town.tsx`; water is `ground` + `solid` without `surface`. `tests/rio_check.lua` validates the sheet; `tests/zona1_check.lua` ("la quebrada") validates the map.

## The course (`river.mjs`)

The centre line is a function row = c(col) in the ORIGINAL row frame (negative = north of the old map). Anchors fix it at the user's distances, measured NORTH from the object's top row to the centre of the water run in that column:
church (door centre col) `RIVER_CHURCH_M` = 175 m (user said 150-200; 87.5 tiles rounded to **88**), cafetal (centre col, north fence row) `RIVER_CAFETAL_M` = 20 m = 10 tiles, casa de mama (centre col, top row) `RIVER_MAMA_M` = 50 m = 25 tiles.
Between anchors: smoothstep baseline (it only moves between the +-8 flat columns around each anchor) + seeded noise (+-5 tiles, wavelengths 27-62) that is zero within `ANCHOR_FLAT` = 8 cols of an anchor and `BRIDGE_FLAT` = 9 cols of the bridge. Left (east) of the church and right (west) of mama's house the baseline stays at the nearest anchor's level.
Width = 4.4 tiles on the flat stretches, 3.2-6 elsewhere (value noise, wavelength 33). The mask is the union of discs along the line, cleaned of slivers (1-tile peninsulas / channels). Result: 4-7 water rows per column, slope <= 0.51 rows per column.
`TOP_PAD` = 6 + 1 - (northernmost water row, original frame) = 93: 6 grass rows north of the northernmost bank everywhere.

## Reshaping the river

Edit `RIVER_*_M`, `OLD_*` anchors or the `makeRiver` call in `zona1_map.mjs`, or the noise terms / widths in `river.mjs`; run `node tools/tilegen/gen.mjs`. `TOP_PAD` and `MAP_H` follow automatically, but update the literal hero start in `scenes/main.lua` (`y = 300 + TOP_PAD * 16`; `gen.mjs` throws if it is stale) and `TOP` in `tests/zona1_check.lua`. `baseReport` throws if a measured distance is off by more than 1 tile.
`--rio-preview <dir>` writes the river crops (x2: church, church with the 88 rows of land, cafetal, mama, bridge day / night, bridge x4, inside the monte day / night, a bend), the whole northern strip and the sheet x4.

## Autotiling

Water cells: `water_a/b` (+ 7 % `water_ripple`), `rio_water_rapids_*` in the fast reaches (value noise along the course), `rio_water_dark` pools. Land cells next to water get a bank tile: one wet side `bank_<side>`, two adjacent wet sides `bank_corner_*`, only a wet diagonal `rio_bank_inner_*`.
Two cells out: `rio_gravel_*` bars, `river_stones`, `riverside_grass`. Forest and scattered trees keep 2 (monte) / 3 (north land) tiles from the water. 55 boulders stand in the stream (denser in the rapids); the water is solid anyway.

## The bridge (N-S asphalt street, cols 260-263)

Chosen where the river is flat and horizontal: cols 257-266 cross the same water rows (9-13). Deck = the 4 asphalt columns + sidewalks 259 / 264 (the street carried straight over the water: those cells simply have no water tile), curb cells on cols 258 / 265 with the guard rails (`rio_rail_*`, solid) and a `rio_post` at each of the 4 ends, piers (`rio_abutment_*`) in the water on cols 257 / 266 at the first, middle and last water row. Bridge rows 6-16 = 2 tiles past each bank row (8 and 14). Pedestrians walk across cols 259-264; water, rails, posts and piers are solid. Collision: every tile layer collides, water is a solid ground tile, so `gen.mjs` verifies that no deck cell is blocked and every other water cell is.

## Zones and checks

Zone `rio` ("La quebrada", ambience `quebrada`): 20-tile strips following the river, each the water of its columns +- 8 rows; the same zone id for all strips, objects named `zone_rio_NN` (the engine reads `properties.zone`; the smaller area wins over `zona1` / `monte`).
`gen.mjs` asserts: the three distances (+-1 tile), >= 6 grass rows north, nothing but boulders / piers on water, water only under the deck, and the flood fill from the church spawn: all old targets reachable, the north bank reachable over the bridge, cut off without it, 0 walkable water cells.
